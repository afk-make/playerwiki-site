Construction of a new bridge over the River Southrun, intended to link the city of Wurchester with the lands to the west, has been temporarily halted following protests from local residents. Opponents of the project have raised concerns over increased migration from the western territories, citing potential pressures on housing, employment and public services. Supporters argue that the crossing would improve trade and travel between the two regions. City officials have yet to announce when construction will resume.
