
Small chapel at No. 2 Oak Road in [[Brunswurf Town|Brunswurf]]. The Priest if [[Father Dorian]].

A regular redbrick building detached, sat on the corner, fields behind it, woods even further.

Inside, **The Greyveil Chapel** is dim and close, the red brick walls left bare and unadorned, giving the space a simple, almost humble feel. A few rows of plain wooden benches face a low stone altar, worn smooth with age, with no grand symbols—just a small, weathered carving set into its surface. The light comes from a handful of candles placed carefully around the room, their glow soft and steady rather than bright. The air is still, carrying a faint scent of ash and old wood, and sound seems to settle quickly, as if the room absorbs it. It feels less like a place of ceremony and more like a place to sit quietly with difficult thoughts.
