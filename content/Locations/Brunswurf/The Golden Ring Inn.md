A large tavern in [[Brunswurf Town|Brunswurf]], located at No. 7 Market Street.

![[LI003 - TheGoldenRingInn.png]]

The Golden Ring Inn dominates its stretch of road with an air of ambition that slightly outpaces its reality. The building is large and sprawling, built of timber and pale plaster, with a wide, arched entrance designed for coaches and wagons to pass through into a cobbled yard beyond.

Lanterns hang in neat rows along the frontage, polished brass fittings catching the light—though a closer look reveals tarnish creeping in at the edges. A painted sign of a gilded ring hangs proudly above the entrance, its gold leaf flaking just enough to betray its age.

Inside, the inn opens into a broad common hall that _tries very hard_ to impress. Long tables and benches fill most of the space, but there are also a few smaller, “private” tables tucked along the walls, separated by half-hearted wooden screens. Someone has attempted elegance—there are chandeliers overhead and decorative wall hangings—but they’re mismatched, slightly crooked, or worn thin from years of use. The floors are well-trodden, the wood dulled by countless boots, and the air is thick with the smell of ale, roasted meat, and too many people in too little space.

The staff move quickly, dressed in uniforms that were clearly meant to look refined—dark vests, pale shirts—but rarely quite fit or stay clean for long. The place is always busy: merchants, travelers, and locals all crammed together, creating a constant hum of noise that occasionally tips into shouting. Upstairs, a long creaking corridor leads to guest rooms of varying quality—some surprisingly comfortable, others little better than a bed and a door that doesn’t quite shut.

Despite its flaws, the **Golden Ring Inn** has a certain charm. It’s dependable, lively, and always full of stories—just not quite as grand as it wants you to believe.
