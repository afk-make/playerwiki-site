An old tavern in [[Brunswurf Town Map|Brunswurf]] located at No. 8 King's Way.
![[LI002 - TheBrokenCross.png]]
The Broken Cross Tavern is a small, black-and-white timbered building, its crooked beams and low roof giving it a slightly leaning, timeworn look. Warm light spills from its small windows, and a simple wooden sign—painted with a cracked cross—hangs above a narrow, well-used doorway.

Inside, the space is tight but inviting, with low ceilings, dark wood, and a constant glow from a modest hearth. It feels less like a business and more like someone’s front room—quiet, close, and comfortably worn by years of familiar faces.

Run by [[Edric Malvern]].
