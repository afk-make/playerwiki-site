A small tavern in [[Brunswurf Town|Brunswurf]] located at No. 20 Market Street. Run by Roric Venn.

![[LI001 - TheBloodCross.png]]

The Blood Cross Tavern is a slim, narrow brick building wedged tightly between two larger shopfronts along the busy market street. Its dark red bricks are worn smooth with age, and the upper floor leans just slightly inward, as if pressed by the buildings on either side. A weathered wooden sign hangs above the door, painted with a deep crimson cross that has long since begun to fade and crack. The entrance is low and easily missed, half-shadowed by the overhang of the neighboring shops and the constant movement of the street. Small, narrow windows glow faintly from within, hinting at a darker, quieter space compared to the bustle outside. It feels tucked away and overlooked—yet somehow always occupied by those who know exactly where to find it.

Inside, the **Blood Cross Tavern** is far deeper than it first appears, stretching back in a long, narrow line from the cramped entrance. The ceiling is low and supported by dark wooden beams, and the space is lit by a scatter of candles that leave the far end in soft shadow. A thin bar runs along one wall, forcing patrons into close quarters, with small tables tucked wherever they can fit. The air is warm and heavy, filled with murmured conversation rather than loud noise. Towards the back, the tavern grows quieter, the light dimmer, and the feeling more private—as if the deeper you go, the less the outside world matters.
