A large tavern and Inn in [[Brunswurf Town|Brunswurf]], located at No. 22 Wurchester Road, run by Melba Fenlock. 

![[LI005 - TheHound&Phoenix.png]]

Set just off the main road in Brunswurf, The Hound & Phoenix is the kind of place travelers remember—and locals pretend not to care about. 

The building itself is a sturdy, timber-framed inn with a slightly sagging roof and a wide stone chimney that never seems to stop smoking. Its sign creaks gently in the wind: a painted black hunting hound staring up at a rising phoenix, the bird’s wings picked out in faded gold leaf that still catches the light at sunset.
