A small tavern in [[Brunswurf Town|Brunswurf]], located at No. 29 Market Street, run by Tobin Hale.

![[LI004 - TheHagsHead.png]]

The Hag’s Head sits at the very end of market street, a small, slightly crooked building that looks older than most around it. Its dark timber frame and worn plaster lean subtly toward the shop on its left, while the ground to the right gives way to a narrow dirt path leading out of town. A weathered sign hangs above the door, carved in the likeness of a crone’s face, its features smoothed by years of wind and rain.

The windows are small and uneven, their glass slightly warped, catching the light in strange ways. A single lantern hangs by the entrance, casting a dim, flickering glow that never quite reaches the path beside it. It feels like the last place in town before the world turns quieter—and a little less certain.

Inside, **The Hag’s Head** feels older than the building outside suggests, as if it has been standing unchanged for generations. The ceiling is low and uneven, with dark beams that seem to press down over a handful of small, mismatched tables. The air smells faintly of herbs, smoke, and something bitter you can’t quite place, drifting from a small hearth tucked at the back. Shelves behind the bar are cluttered with odd bottles and jars, some filled with cloudy liquids or dried things that don’t look entirely like food. The light is dim and amber, coming from a few candles and the hearth, leaving corners of the room in soft shadow. It’s quiet—not empty, but hushed.
