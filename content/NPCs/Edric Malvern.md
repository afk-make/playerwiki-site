Owner of the [[The Broken Cross]] in [[Brunswurf Town Map|Brunswurf]].
![[NPC004 - EdricMalvern.png]]


Edric is a compact, broad-built man in his early fifties, with thinning sandy hair and a thick, neatly kept moustache that’s gone more grey than gold. His face is lined but kind, with steady blue eyes that miss very little, even when he pretends not to be paying attention. He wears a simple, clean shirt with rolled sleeves and a well-worn waistcoat, always with a cloth tucked into his belt for wiping down the bar.

Knows a bit about Adventurer's Guilds and how to apply.
