# A Brief History of the Kingdom of Arvenia

The Kingdom of Arvenia lies within a small chain of islands to the northwest of the great continent. Though the kingdom has enjoyed an unbroken line of hereditary monarchs for the past 1,459 years, the history of its people stretches back many thousands of years.

## The Fall of Bramselden

Long before the founding of the modern kingdom, a wealthy and advanced civilisation flourished upon the islands. At its heart stood the great city of **Bramselden**, built upon the site of what is now Bramhold. It was a centre of wealth, learning and culture, and the seat of power for a civilisation whose achievements have never been fully understood.

Approximately two thousand years ago, Bramselden's civilisation suffered a sudden and catastrophic collapse. The precise date and cause remain unknown, and scholars continue to debate what brought about its downfall. Some believe it was the result of internal conflict, while others suggest famine, disease or invasion. Whatever the cause, the consequences were devastating.

With the collapse of central authority, the islands descended into anarchy. Countless lives were lost, settlements were abandoned, and many fled their ancestral homes. Sensing opportunity, peoples from neighbouring lands crossed the seas to seize territory for themselves.

For centuries, the islands remained divided. Warring tribes, foreign invaders and rival warlords fought for control of the land. The prosperity of Bramselden became little more than a distant memory, and much of the knowledge of the old civilisation was lost.

## The Rise of Edmond

It was during this age of turmoil, some 1,467 years ago, that a man known only as **Edmond** emerged.

Little is known of his origins. His birthplace, family and early life remain a mystery, and much of what is recorded of him comes from accounts written generations after his death.

Edmond travelled across the divided lands, gathering followers and persuading rival tribes to unite beneath a common cause. He spoke of restoring order, driving out foreign occupiers and establishing a single kingdom in which the people of the islands might once again prosper.

Those who joined him became part of a growing army. Those who refused were conquered, and those who resisted were destroyed. Through diplomacy, force and relentless determination, Edmond united much of the fractured land under his command.

As his influence grew, so too did his ambition. No longer content merely to defend his followers, Edmond sought to reclaim the islands from those who had invaded them during the centuries of chaos.

His armies marched across the land, defeating rival factions and driving foreign forces steadily southward. With each victory, more settlements pledged their allegiance, and the dream of a united kingdom began to take shape.

At the height of his campaign, Edmond was crowned **King Edmond**, the first monarch of a newly united Arvenia.

## The Battle of the Thyne

King Edmond's campaign eventually drove the remaining invaders south to the River Thyne, where they gathered for a final defence.

Determined to expel them from the islands entirely, Edmond led his army into battle upon the marshlands bordering the river.

The fighting was fierce, and it was here that the king met his end. During the battle, Edmond was struck by a shower of arrows and fell upon the marshy ground.

Though their king had been slain, his army continued the assault. The invaders were defeated and driven back towards the sea, securing the victory for which Edmond had fought.

His followers recovered his body and laid him to rest upon the very ground where he had fallen. A shrine was erected over his resting place, marking the death of the man who had united the kingdom.

## The Founding of the Edmondson Dynasty

Despite Edmond's achievements, his death left the young kingdom without a secure succession. Rival claimants emerged, and several years of internal conflict followed as factions struggled for control of the throne.

Eventually, Edmond's legitimate son, **Karolos**, prevailed and was crowned **King Karolos Edmondson**, establishing the royal dynasty that continues to rule Arvenia to this day.

Under Karolos and his successors, the kingdom entered a period of stability and prosperity. Around the shrine of King Edmond, a settlement began to grow. Over generations, this settlement expanded into a great city, eventually becoming known as **Lundhelm**.

As the seat of the Edmondson monarchy, Lundhelm grew in wealth, influence and importance, ultimately becoming the capital of Arvenia and one of the most prosperous cities in the known world.

For 1,459 years, the throne of Arvenia has passed through an unbroken hereditary line of House Edmondson. Wars have been fought, borders have shifted, and generations have come and gone, yet the dynasty has endured.

Today, the kingdom is ruled from Lundhelm by **His Majesty King Edran IV, Edran Edmondson**, a direct descendant of the kingdom's founding monarch.

## The Prophecy of Edmond

Among the people of Arvenia, an ancient legend has survived through the centuries. Its origins are uncertain, though some claim the words were first spoken by those who stood beside Edmond upon the marshlands of the Thyne.

The legend holds that the fate of the kingdom is bound to the bloodline of its founder.

_“So long as the blood of Edmond sits upon the throne, Arvenia shall endure. But upon the day his line is broken, the kingdom shall fall.”_

Whether this is an ancient prophecy, a warning passed down through generations, or merely a tale encouraged by the royal family to strengthen its claim to power remains a matter of debate.

# The Peoples of Arvenia

The Kingdom of Arvenia is predominantly inhabited by humans, who make up the great majority of its population. However, centuries of trade, diplomacy, migration and occasional conflict have brought many other peoples to its shores.

While human settlements dominate the landscape, it is not uncommon to encounter gnomes, dwarves and members of other races, particularly in the kingdom's larger towns, cities and trading ports.

## The Gnomes of the Western Principalities

To the west of Arvenia lie the **Western Principalities**, a collection of smaller kingdoms, territories and independent settlements inhabited predominantly by gnomes. Each maintains its own traditions and systems of government, with authority resting in the hands of kings, rulers, chiefs or local councils.

The relationship between Arvenia and the Western Principalities has not always been peaceful. Centuries of diplomacy, negotiation and countless treaties and trade agreements have gradually established a lasting peace between the neighbouring lands.

Today, gnomes are a familiar sight throughout Arvenia. Many have settled permanently within the kingdom, establishing businesses, practising skilled trades and becoming valued members of their communities. Some have risen to positions of considerable influence in politics, government and even the royal court.

Among the human population, gnomes are generally received with curiosity and good humour, although such attention is not always welcomed by the gnomes themselves.

Despite the long-standing peace, resentment persists among certain communities within the Western Principalities. Some believe that successive agreements have disproportionately favoured Arvenia, granting the larger kingdom excessive influence over trade and political affairs. Critics argue that their own rulers have surrendered too much in exchange for peace and prosperity.

These grievances remain a source of occasional political tension, although relations between the neighbouring peoples are generally peaceful.

## The Dwarves of the Northern Reaches

Far to the north lie the **Northern Reaches**, a vast and rugged region of towering mountains, deep valleys and harsh, unforgiving terrain.

The people of Arvenia have rarely ventured far beyond the River Lyver, which marks the southern approach to these northern lands. Instead, contact with the dwarven inhabitants has traditionally been maintained through trade with settlements situated at the lower altitudes.

Relations between Arvenia and the dwarven peoples have a long and sometimes troubled history. Records dating back to the establishment of the Edmondson dynasty describe periods of warfare and territorial conflict, leaving grievances that have endured across generations.

Although open hostilities have long since diminished, a degree of mutual suspicion remains, particularly among more traditional communities on either side.

Nevertheless, dwarves are far from uncommon within Arvenia. Their expertise in mining, metallurgy, ironworking and other skilled trades has made them important participants in the kingdom's economy. Dwarven merchants, smiths and craftsmen can be found in many of Arvenia's towns and cities, particularly within its industrial centres.

Beyond the known settlements of the lower mountains, much of the Northern Reaches remains unexplored by Arvenian travellers. The full extent of the dwarven territories, and what may lie farther north, is largely unknown to the people of the kingdom.

## The Elves of the Eastern Continent

Across the sea to the southeast lies the great continent, home to numerous kingdoms, cultures and peoples.

Among the closest to Arvenia is an established Elven kingdom, with which contact has been maintained through maritime trade and diplomatic exchange.

Although elves remain a relatively small minority within Arvenia, their presence is not considered particularly unusual, especially in major ports and cities. Half-elves are more frequently encountered, with some families having lived within the kingdom for generations.

In smaller rural communities, however, both elves and half-elves are considerably less common, and their arrival may still attract curiosity.

## The Orcs of the Northeast

The northeastern regions of the great continent are home to numerous orc tribes and settlements.

Historically, contact between these peoples and Arvenia was limited. However, in recent years, new trade agreements and improved relations have opened routes between the kingdom and several orc communities.

As a result, increasing numbers of orcs have begun travelling to Arvenia, seeking opportunities in trade, employment and commerce. While their numbers remain relatively small, their presence has become increasingly noticeable in the kingdom's larger towns and cities.

These developments have been welcomed by many, who regard closer relations with the orc tribes as a positive step towards greater cooperation, prosperity and understanding between peoples.

Others have expressed concern about the pace of these changes, questioning the economic and social consequences of increased migration and foreign trade.

In some cities, these disagreements have led to public demonstrations, political disputes and growing tensions between communities.

The matter has become an increasingly prominent subject of public debate, reflecting wider differences of opinion concerning Arvenia's relationship with the peoples beyond its borders.

For most inhabitants, however, daily life continues much as it has for generations, with people of different backgrounds living, working and trading alongside one another throughout the kingdom.
